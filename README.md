# Where Next

Where next is an app created for those who love to travel and document their adventures online in the style of creating blogs. 

This app gives users the chance to create a profile, write blogs after their travels, and publish it online. You can also follow other creators on this platform and follow their journeys wherever they go around the world. 

You can connect with other users online by following them, liking their posts, leaving comments, suggestions or tips, and possibly creating  friendships where people can meet up and travel to a new destination together.
