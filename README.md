# Where Next

WhereNext is a social travel blogging platform where users document their journeys, share experiences, and contribute their practical advice. It allows travelers to connect with fellow explorers worldwide, create beautifully detailed travel blogs, follow inspiring creators, and build meaningful, lasting travel connections. 

We are full-stack developers from a Health Informatics background, and we've seen firsthand how disconnected information sources overwhelm users—whether in healthcare or travel planning. That's why we're building WhereNext: to solve that problem by unifying scattered insights into a single, community-powered platform designed to turn inspiration into action. 

Our mission is to transform travel from overwhelming research into confident adventure, fostering authentic connections between travelers across the globe. 

On WhereNext, you can connect with other users by following them, liking their posts, leaving comments, suggestions, or tips, and forming genuine friendships that can lead to meeting up and exploring new destinations together.
